# Code review, 8c405e56 (0.8.0) to 7891345f, 2026-10-17

Six read-only reviewers covered the Qwen family, GLM, Nemotron Bolt, the two LFM2 families with the shared tool-call
parsers, the operations layer and the two gateway branches. This file is the triage. A fix may not change replies
(drafted equals serial, resumed equals fresh, golden checks hold) or lower speed. Items marked "later" change
numerics or need a larger design, and go to TASKS.md instead.

Every finding triaged "to fix now" was fixed by 18 Oct 2026, and the Fixed table names its commit. The gateway
and check-eval fixes sit on two unmerged branches of `~/mini-llm-gateway`, `feat/empty-retry` and `feat/check-eval`.
